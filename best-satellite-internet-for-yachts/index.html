Best Satellite Internet for Yachts (2026)

Independent comparison · last updated November 2026 · Alcyone Network

Yacht connectivity has changed faster in two years than in the previous twenty. Low-Earth-orbit (LEO) networks now deliver near-fibre speeds offshore, while traditional VSAT still wins where guaranteed, pole-to-pole uptime is non-negotiable. This guide compares the three real options in 2026 — Starlink Maritime, VSAT, and OneWeb/LEO via service providers — so owners, captains, fleet managers and technical teams can match the right system to how the vessel is actually used. The three options in 2026

1. Starlink Maritime (LEO) — Best for coastal cruising, liveaboards and charter

  • Low latency (25–60 ms) and high speed (50–220 Mbps on the Flat High Performance antenna). (Source: MaritLink, Sept 2026)
  • Near-global coverage, but with occasional mid-ocean gaps and no coverage above ~60° latitude. (Source: Earth SIMs 6-week yacht test, 2026)
  • Business SLA of 99% (hardware failures only); Roam/residential has no contractual SLA.

2. VSAT (GEO — e.g. Intelsat, delivered via Marlink, Speedcast) — Best for guaranteed uptime, polar routes and deep-ocean crossings

  • Higher latency (600–800 ms) and lower typical speed (~5–15 Mbps), but uninterrupted, pole-to-pole coverage.
  • Enterprise SLA of 99.5–99.9% with financial penalties and 24/7 support. (Source: Earth SIMs, 2026)

3. OneWeb / Eutelsat (LEO via service providers) — An emerging alternative

Sold through maritime integrators (Marlink, Speedcast, Station Satcom, Castor Marine) rather than direct, and increasingly paired with VSAT in hybrid LEO+GEO setups for redundancy. (Sources: SuperyachtNews; Marine Link, 2026)

4. Hybrid (Starlink + VSAT) — What most superyachts and charter operations now choose

LEO for everyday speed and cost, VSAT as the always-on fallback.

How to choose (by profile)

  • Coastal cruising / liveaboard → Starlink Maritime (Local or Global Priority).
  • Ocean crossings / high latitudes / guaranteed uptime → VSAT, or hybrid.
  • Charter / superyacht with heavy guest demand → Hybrid (Starlink for bandwidth + VSAT for reliability).
